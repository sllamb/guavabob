Guava Bob’s Grande Adventures

A storm forces Bob to leave his tree but Juan’s 4-wheeler gets stuck in the mud. So Bob has to steer while Juan pushes from behind. They eventually make it back to Juan’s house where Bob meets Nico and Emilia and becomes part of the familia. Bob gets the confidence to join Juan on future adventures.

Guava Bob Flies a Kite

Bob reluctantly leaves his coconut tree to help Juan's ninos, Emilia and Nico, prepare for a local kite-flying competition by the Caribbean castle. When decorating their kites, Bob finds himself getting painted as much as the kites. Once the competition starts, Nico’s kite gets stuck on the castle walls, and Bob finds himself flying through the air and to victory.

Guava Bob & the Twinkling Lagoon

To comfort a nervous Emilia, Bob joins the family on a nighttime kayaking tour to a magical, glowing lagoon. While helping the kids learn to paddle and attempting to retrieve a lost oar, Bob takes an unexpected tumble into the water. Bob discovers that he, too, can magically twinkle in the dark easing Emilia’s fears of the dark.
