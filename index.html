Currently querying agents to publish the following books:

Guava Bob’s Grande Adventures

When a tropical storm forces Bob from his cozy tree, his peaceful life is flipped upside down! Stranded in the pouring rain, Juan’s four-wheeler gets hopelessly stuck in an island mudslide. To help them reach safety, the reluctant lizard must step up to the steering wheel while Juan pushes from behind. Buckle up for a muddy, slippery rescue mission that leads Bob straight to Juan's house, where a warm welcome from Nico and Emilia proves that finding your familia is the greatest adventure of all!

Guava Bob Flies a Kite

Let's go fly a kite with Bob as he trades his relaxing coconut tree for a colorful kite-flying competition by a grand Caribbean castle! Helping Nico and Emilia decorate their kites is a messy business, and Bob ends up wearing almost as much paint as the paper. But when the big race begins and Nico's kite gets disastrously snagged high up on the castle walls, Bob must take a leap of faith to free it. Catch the wind and fly along on a high-altitude rescue mission where a little lizard bravery saves the day!

Guava Bob & the Twinkling Lagoon

Paddle into the night with Bob as he joins the family on a kayaking tour to a magical, glowing lagoon! while helping the kids learn to paddle and attempting to retrieve a lost oar, Bob takes an unexpected tumble into the water. Instead of a scary splash, Bob discovers a brilliant surprise—the magical water makes him twinkle and glow in the dark! Grab your lifejacket and glide into a breathtaking, sparkly journey that shows Emilia there is nothing to fear in the dark.

Guava Bob Hunts for Treasure

Join Bob on a treasure hunt tour with Nico and Emilia to an island where pirates were rumoured to have buried their loot! On their search for gold and jewels, they end up discovering "mohawk" lizards who Bob assumes are out to run away with the treasure. But when their newly unearthed pirate booty gets swept out to sea, Bob must figure out if these lightning-fast reptiles are truly foes—or exactly the friends they need. Grab your plastic sword and dive into a wild beach adventure where the greatest treasures aren't always buried in the sand!
