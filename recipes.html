Guava Baked Donuts

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter melted
  • 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup powdered sugar (for glaze)
  • 1 tablespoon guava fruit spread (for glaze)
  • 1 tablespoon milk (for glaze)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and lightly grease a donut pan.
  2. Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
  4. Pour wet ingredients into dry and stir until just combined.
  5. Pipe batter into pan, filling 2/3 full.
  6. Bake 11 to 14 minutes, let cool in pan, then transfer to a wire rack.
  7. For glaze, whisk powdered sugar, guava spread, and 1 tablespoon milk until smooth.
  8. Dip cooled donuts into glaze and let set.

Guava Puff Pastry

Ingredients

  • Puff Pastry
  • Guava fruit spread
  • Powdered Sugar

Instructions

  1. Prepare a baking sheet with baking paper on top
  2. Defrost the puff pastry
  3. Divide into two layers and lay one on top of the other
  4. Using a pizza cutter, cut into 9 even pieces
  5. Put two teaspoons of guava fruit spread onto one layer of the puff pastry
  6. Cover each piece with the corresponding layer
  7. Bake at 400 degrees for 15 minutes
  8. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and let it cool before eating because the guava inside can stay quite hot!

Guava Lemonade

Ingredients

  • Guava Nectar - one fourth cup
  • Guava sparkling juice - one fourth cup sparkling guava
  • Water - one third cup water
  • Lemon Juice - 1 tablespoon

Instructions

Mix all ingredients and chill in refrigerator and serve cold

Tropical Guava Smoothie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen guava cubes
  • 1/2 cup fresh or frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1/2 cup plain or vanilla Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup guava nectar

Instructions

  1. Add the frozen guava cubes, pineapple chunks, Greek yogurt, and guava nectar to a blender.
  2. Blend on high until completely smooth and creamy.
  3. If the smoothie is too thick, add a splash more guava nectar until it reaches your desired consistency.
  4. Pour into a tall glass and enjoy a taste of the tropics!

Guava Acai Bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 packet (100g) frozen unsweetened acai puree
  • 1/2 cup frozen guava chunks
  • 1/2 frozen banana
  • 1/4 cup guava nectar or apple juice
  • Toppings: Granola, sliced kiwi, fresh mango, coconut flakes

Instructions

  1. Break the acai packet into pieces and add it to your blender.
  2. Add the frozen guava chunks, frozen banana, and guava nectar.
  3. Blend on a low speed, using a tamper if necessary, until thick and smooth (it should have a scoopable consistency).
  4. Spoon the thick blend into a bowl.
  5. Arrange your favorite tropical fruit toppings, coconut flakes, and granola neatly on top. Grab a spoon and dive in!
