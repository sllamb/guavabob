Our Inspiration

On a family vacation to Liquillo Puerto Rico, the author opened the balcony to find an iguana resting in a palm tree above an orange sand beach. His kids were content to stay inside and watch one Mr Beast video after another in spite of the amazing activities just outside their door.


He told his wife that he felt like a tour guide who had to sell them on an adventure worthy of putting down their devices. To convince them to try some new activities, they bribed them with delicious tropical pastries from a bakery in Old San Juan which included guava cakes. During nightly bedtime stories on that wonderful island vacation, the idea for a guava eating lizard named Bob and his travel adventures came to life!
